Sivia's parents: Joseph Hausman (H-020) and Rose Shapiro (H-021)

  • Rose's crossing. Ellis Island, SS Ebro from Havana, Cuba, arrived 12 May 1925, List 4, line 9. The record says: "Rejza Hujzman", 60, Hebrew, widow, born Poland at "Brassc" (probably Brześć, i.e. Brest-Litovsk), last residence Havana. Nearest relative left behind: "Miss Fega Hujzman (daughter), San Ignacio 57, Havana". Going to join "G. Weisberg (daughter), 636 E. 135th St, New York City" (Bronx; street read from the image). Held for special inquiry as likely to become a public charge, with a medical note about senility; the detention sheet has "Hausman, Reise" written above her name; admitted about 14 May 1925.
  • Conclusions: this is Sylvia's mother (medium-high): the name (Reise = Rose), age, widowhood, a US-resident daughter, and an arrival three weeks before the 1925 census all fit. Joseph Hausman had died before May 1925, probably in Europe or Cuba. Sylvia had at least two sisters: Fega (in Havana in 1925) and G. Weisberg (Bronx). Rose's birthplace "Brassc" suggests the Hausmans came from Brest-Litovsk.
  • Rose came to America in 1925 (RZ-S55): in the 1925 New York census she is "Hausman, Rose" (indexed "Haresman"), 62 (born about 1863), mother-in-law in Morris's household at 537 Georgia Ave, alien, 3 weeks in the US. Joseph does not appear in any family household 1915–1930, so he may have died in Europe or stayed behind.

  • The Bronx Rose Hausman who died in 1932 is her: a Bronx death fits a daughter living at 636 E. 135th St: age 67 in 1932 fits born about 1863–65; "7 years in US" fits a 1925 arrival; her father is recorded as "William Sapir(?)", and Sapir is a form of Shapiro. See below.

  • Not yet found. Known only from the 1915 marriage certificate (RZ-S01).

  • The NYC death index (RZ-S04) to 1948 has candidates for both names in the Bronx and Manhattan, but none in Brooklyn.
  • Rose Hausman (RZ-S12) died in the Bronx on 15 Nov 1932, age 67, of 804 Forest Ave. Her parents were William Sapir(?) and Hannah Katz; 7 years in the US. Sylvia's mother (high; see above). Still to read: the informant and burial details.
  • Sylvia's own 1910 crossing was not found: Ellis Island starts-with and sounds-like searches 1908–1912 (Hausm, Hauzm, Gausm, Chausm, Hauss, Hosm…), about 15 first-name forms, a Brest town filter, Ancestry New York lists, and the 1915 New York census. Possible that she travelled under a quite different name, or via Cuba like her mother and sister. A 17-year-old "Marjein Hausmann" from "Wokow" (SS Lapland, 5 Dec 1910) is unverified.
