Meyer Rodilitz

b. c. 1894

AboutFamilyResearch notes

Meyer (Meier), born on 5 March 1894 in Kobrin, came from Rotterdam on the Rotterdam in July 1911. He was in Europe at the end of the First World War and returned to New York in November 1919. He married Henrietta Frankel in 1932 and died in 1950.
