Catherine Saslow (Zaslow)

1891–1937

AboutFamilyResearch notes

Catherine (Katie; Hebrew Beila) Saslow was born in April 1891 in Russia and came to America as Beile, aged one, with her mother and sister on the Circassia in 1893. In 1910, a hosiery worker of about 19, she married Benjamin Zaslow. They had four children, the youngest Doris. She died on 5 September 1937, aged 46, and is buried at Har Jehuda beside her father.

Catherine (Z-011): Doris's mother, and the Saslow family

1900 census. Philadelphia Ward 3, ED 59, sheet 6B, lines 61–67, 787 S. 3rd St, dwelling 93, family 113, enumerated 4 Jun 1900. The record says: - Benjamin Saslow, head, born Jun 1865, 34, married 13 years, born Russia, immigrated 1892, alien, paper hanger, reads, writes and speaks English, rents. - Bessie, wife, born Mar 1867, 33; 6 children born, 5 living; immigrated 1893; cannot read, write or speak English. - Beckie, daughter, born Dec 1889, Russia, immigrated 1893. - Katie, daughter, born Apr 1891, Russia, immigrated 1893, at school. This is Catherine. - Louis (Jul 1893), Herman (Nov 1896) and Mollie (Apr 1899), all born in Pennsylvania. Also boarders named Udefzky. No Gold or Weiss in the house. - (Ancestry's index misreads "Paper Hanger" as "Paper Keeper" and Bessie's literacy as yes.)

Marriage: Philadelphia marriage licence no. 260214, 1910: groom "Beny Zaslof", bride "Katie Saslow". The index gives only the year; with the 1910 census (Katie single on 15 Apr) the wedding was between mid-April and December 1910. The licence and application, which name both sets of parents and birthplaces, are not online; they are held by the Philadelphia Register of Wills.

1910 census (RZ-S28): indexed as "Sarlow", 1924 Fifth St, Ward 39. Benjamin 41, married 23 years, immigrated 1892, naturalized, paper hanger with his own store. Bessie ("Ressie") 40, immigrated 1893. Kate 17, single, at home, hosiery operator, so she married Benjamin Zaslow after census day (15 Apr 1910). Benjamin Zaslow himself was not found in 1910 under any spelling tried. 1920 (RZ-S29): Benjamin Saslow still gives 1892.

Benjamin Saslow's naturalization (RZ-S30, RZ-S31): declaration 26 Oct 1900, signed "B. Saslaw(?)", of 787 S 3rd St; naturalized 15 Dec 1902 (no. 9235). 1901 city directory: "Benj Saslow, paperhanger" and "Bessie, grocer", both at 787 S 3d.

Son Louis (RZ-S34, RZ-S35): WWI draft card gives his birth as 25 Sep 1895 (not Jul 1893 as in 1900); his 1965 Pennsylvania death certificate names his parents as Benjamin and Bessie.

The 1893 crossing (RZ-S06, RZ-S32, RZ-S33). Hamburg emigration list, ship Breslau, 16 Jun 1893: Leib (30, labourer), Basche (26, wife), Jety (3), Beile (1), from "Podolsk", bound for Philadelphia. Then SS Circassia, Glasgow to Ellis Island, 3 Jul 1893: Leib 30 (printer), Basche 26, Jety 2, Beile 1.

Gravestones. Har Jehuda Cemetery, Upper Darby. The Hebrew inscriptions say: - Benjamin Saslow: "Dov son of R. David", died 11 Av 5697 (19 Jul 1937), age 73. - Bessie Saslow: "Batya daughter of R. Aharon", died 16 Iyar 5696 (8 May 1936). - Catherine Zaslow: "Beila daughter of R. Dov", died 29 Elul 5697 (5 Sep 1937), age 46. - Beckie's stone at Har Nebo is fallen and worn; no Hebrew legible.

Burial register (RZ-S58). The Har Judea Cemetery register (Ancestry collection 2451) has Benjamin Saslow (buried 21 Jul 1937, grave 23) and Catherine (indexed "Katie Saslow", buried 8 Sep 1937, grave 22) side by side in section D, line 7-1, in the plot of the "Tulchiner" society, a society of people from Tulchin (Tulchyn), Podolia. The register gives Benjamin's age as 61 (his stone says 73).

Benjamin Saslow's naturalization (RZ-S30, RZ-S31). Circuit Court, Eastern District of Pennsylvania. Declaration 26 Oct 1900, aged 34, 787 S 3rd St, signed "B. Saslaw"; petition and oath 15 Dec 1902, no. 9235, signed "Benjaman [his X mark] Saslow", witness "Osher Ginsburg" (reading uncertain) of 786 S. 2nd St. No birthplace, ship or date (the pre-1906 forms didn't ask).

The 1923 witness. On Benjamin Zaslow's 1923 petition the witness is "B. Saslow", paperhanger, of 2208 S. [street], signed "B. Saslaw", the same signature as in 1900. The 1920 census has Benjamin Saslow, paperhanger, owning 2208 South Fifth St. So the witness was Catherine's father (high). (Baby Mildred's 1924 death certificate also reads 2208 So 5th St; an earlier note here said 8th St, which was a misreading.)

Conclusions - Catherine's father was Benjamin Saslow (high), Hebrew name Dov son of David. the compiler’s tree is right on this point. - Catherine's Hebrew name was Beila, so she is the "Beile", aged 1, of the 1893 lists, and her mother Batya is "Basche". The 1893 party is Bessie with Beckie (Jety, 3) and Catherine (Beile, 1): high, about 90%. - "Leib Zaslaw", 30, printer, literate, was probably not Benjamin (medium, about 75%): Benjamin was Dov, a paperhanger who signed by mark, and he named a son Leib (Louis) in 1895, which custom avoided for a living father. Probably a Zaslaw relative escorting the family, or a family passport in another man's name. On the Circassia list Basche is written first. A Zipre Saslaimer ("Lipre Saslainer"), 20, from Podolsk, sailed on the same voyage and may be a relative (low). - Home town: Tulchin, Podolia is now the best lead for the Saslows (medium, from the burial society). Tulchin (Bratslav district) and Obodovka (Olgopol district), Benjamin Zaslow's town, lie about 42 km apart in southern Podolia, so both of Doris's parents' families came from the same corner of Podolia. - Probable link between the two Benjamins: the witness "B. Saslow", a paperhanger, on Benjamin Zaslow's 1923 naturalization petition (RZ-S23) is very probably Catherine's father, Benjamin Saslow the paperhanger, vouching for his son-in-law. (Coordinator's conclusion; the witness's address, 2208 [street unclear], is still to compare.) - Origin: "Podolsk" on both 1893 lists points to Podolia, the same province as Benjamin Zaslow's Obodovka.
