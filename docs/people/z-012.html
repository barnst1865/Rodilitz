Beckie Saslow (Mitchell)

1889–1914

AboutFamilyResearch notes

Beckie (Jety), born in December 1889 in Russia, came with her mother on the Circassia in 1893. She married David Mitchell and died on 26 August 1914, aged 25.

In the family history: The Saslows of Podolia
