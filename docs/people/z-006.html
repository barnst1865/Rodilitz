Miriam Lesnoff (Zaslow)

1848–1931

AboutFamilyResearch notes

Miriam (Marjem, Mary) Zaslow, née Lesnoff, daughter of Herman and Rose Lesnoff, came with her husband Jacob on the Umbria in 1906. In 1910 she had had nine children, seven of them living. She died in Brooklyn on 14 September 1931 after a fall and is buried beside Jacob at Montefiore Cemetery.
