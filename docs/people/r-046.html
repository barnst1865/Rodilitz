Sarah Rodilitz (Gordon)

b. 1907

AboutFamilyResearch notes

Sarah (Sure), the youngest, was born on 6 February 1907 in Kobrin and came with her mother on the Pretoria in 1913. She married Martin Gordon in 1929.
