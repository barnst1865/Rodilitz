Bessie Weiss (Saslow)

1867–1936

AboutFamilyRecordsResearch notes

Bessie (Basche; Hebrew Batya), born in March 1867, was the daughter of Harry (Aharon) Weiss and Molly Gold. A year after her husband, she left Hamburg in June 1893 with her daughters Jety and Beile, crossed from Glasgow on the Circassia, and landed at Ellis Island on 3 July 1893. In 1901 she was a grocer. She died on 8 May 1936 at her daughter Catherine’s house and is buried at Har Jehuda.
