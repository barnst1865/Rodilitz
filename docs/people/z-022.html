Leib Zaslaw

b. c. 1863

AboutFamilyRecordsResearch notes

Leib Zaslaw, 30, a printer who could read and write, travelled with Basche, Jety and Beile in 1893. He was probably a relative escorting the family, not Bessie’s husband.
