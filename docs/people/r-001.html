Theodore Rodilitz

1919–2004

AboutFamilyRecordsResearch notes

Theodore Rodilitz was born in Brooklyn on 25 April 1919, the son of Morris Rodilitz and Sylvia Hausman, and grew up at 537 Georgia Avenue. When he registered for the draft in 1940 he worked for the Comet Manufacturing Company in Manhattan. He enlisted in the Army in April 1941. In 1948, then in radio production, he took out a marriage licence with Doris Zaslow. By 1968 he and Doris had made their home in Anaheim, California. He died on 11 January 2004, aged 84.
