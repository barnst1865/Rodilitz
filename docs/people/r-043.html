Ida Rodilitz (Hovelin)

b. 1898

AboutFamilyResearch notes

Ida (Chaje), born on 19 January 1898 by her father’s petition, came with her mother on the Pretoria in 1913. She married Irving Hovelin in 1927.

In the family history: The Rodilitzes of Kobrin district
