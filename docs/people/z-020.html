Benjamin Saslow

1865–1937

AboutFamilyResearch notes

Benjamin Saslow, Hebrew Dov son of David, was born in June 1865 and reached America in 1892. A paperhanger with his own business, he lived in South Philadelphia, from about 1920 in his own house at 2208 South Fifth Street. He was naturalized in 1902. He died on 19 July 1937 and is buried at Har Jehuda in the plot of a Tulchiner society.
