David Saslow

AboutFamilyResearch notes

David is known from his son Benjamin Saslow’s gravestone, “Dov son of R. David.” He was probably the Duvid Berkovich Zaslava, aged 46, recorded in Tulchin in the 1875 census, registered in the town of Soroki in Bessarabia, with his sons Berko, 12, and Avrum, 3. That identification is new research, not yet in the printed history.
