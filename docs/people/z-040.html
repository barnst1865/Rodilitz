David (Duvid) Zaslaver

AboutFamilyResearch notes

Duvid, son of Berko Zaslaver, born about 1829, married Khasya Dvoyra Nemirovskaya in Tulchin on 14 January 1847, when he was 18 and she 17. Their children included Khaya Sima (1859), Ber (Benjamin Saslow, 1863), Bluma (about 1866), Tsipa (1869) and Avrum (1871). In the 1875 Jewish census he was living in Tulchin, a townsman of Soroki in Bessarabia, with his sons Berko, 12, and Avrum, 3; Soroki kept its own copy of the entry. The family was also registered as colonists of Dombroveny, a Jewish farming colony near Soroki founded in 1836 by settlers from Podolia. Benjamin Saslow’s gravestone calls him “Dov son of R. David.”
