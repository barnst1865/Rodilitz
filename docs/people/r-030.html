Abraham Prozansky

AboutFamilyRecordsResearch notes

Abraham Prozansky (Hebrew Avraham), Mary Rodilitz’s father, is named on her death certificate and on her gravestone. His great-grandson Abraham Jonas Rodolitz, born in 1920, was probably named after him, which suggests he had died in Europe by then.
