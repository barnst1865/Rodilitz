Benjamin Zaslow

1885–1940

AboutFamilyResearch notes

Benjamin Zaslow (Hebrew Dov Ber) was born on 8 January 1885 at “Obodofsky,” Obodovka in Podolia, by his own account. He swore he landed at Philadelphia from Liverpool about 15 March 1904; he was probably the “Berl Saslawer” of the Haverford, and his parents were probably Jacob and Miriam Zaslawer of Brooklyn. In 1910 he married Catherine Saslow in Philadelphia. He became a grocer and kept a fruit store at 2163 East Cambria Street, and was naturalized in 1924. He died on 29 February 1940 and is buried at Har Jehuda.

Benjamin Zaslow (Z-010): Doris's father

Naturalization papers. US District Court, Eastern District of Pennsylvania. The record says: - Declaration of intention no. 67614, 9 Nov 1921: Benjamin Zaslow, 36, grocer, of 2163 E. Cambria St, Philadelphia. Born 8 Jan 1885 at "Obodofsky", Russia; last foreign residence the same. Emigrated from Liverpool on "the vessel Rappaport"; arrived at the port of [Phila., under a stamp] on or about 15 Mar 1904. Wife "Katie", born Russia. - Petition no. 62333, 19 Nov 1923: born 8 Jan 1885, "Obodof[sky]"; left England about 6 Mar 1904; arrived Philadelphia 15 Mar 1904, vessel "Rappaport". Wife "Kate", born 8 Mar 1891(?) in Russia. Children Sylvia, born 22 Dec 1911, and Edward, born 30 Dec 1918, both Philadelphia (Doris was born after the petition). Witnesses: Charles E.(?) Ohrenstein and B.(?) Saslow, probably a paperhanger, probably a relative of Catherine. No certificate of arrival (none was required for arrivals before 1906). - Naturalized 10 Mar 1924, certificate no. 1953580.

1930 census (RZ-S24): "Beniyam" Zaslow, 46, at 2163 E. Cambria St, owner, fruit-store dealer, immigrated 1904, naturalized, age at first marriage 26 (so about 1910); wife Catherine 38; children Sylva 18, Edward 11, Doris 3. This ties the naturalized Benjamin to Doris.

Death. Pennsylvania death certificate no. 13632: Benjamin Zaslow died 29 Feb 1940 at the Jewish Hospital, Philadelphia; of 2163 E. Cambria St; grocer; "widower of Catherine Zaslow"; father Jacob Zaslow, mother Miriam, both born Russia; age given as 46 (which would mean born about 1894, against 1885 in his own papers; prefer his own papers); lung cancer. Buried Har Jehuda Cemetery, Upper Darby, 3 Mar 1940. Informant Louis Zaslow, same address (perhaps Catherine's brother Louis Saslow; unconfirmed). So the "1959" in the member trees is wrong: the 1959 Exponent obituary (RZ-S51) is a different Benjamin Zaslow.

A daughter who died young (RZ-S47): Mildred Zaslow, born about Dec 1923, died 16 Feb 1924 aged 2 months, daughter of Benjamin and Katie, at 2208 S. 5th St, the Saslow grandparents' house (corrected reading; see the Saslow section).

Conclusions - Home town: "Obodofsky" is almost certainly Obodovka (Obodivka), a Jewish town in Olgopol district, Podolia province (now Vinnytsia oblast, Ukraine). This is the research’s identification, medium confidence. Catherine's family on the 1893 manifest also came from "Padolsk" (Podolia), which fits a Podolian marriage network but proves nothing yet. - Arrival: Philadelphia, 15 March 1904, from Liverpool, as he swore. The ship name "Rappaport" is not a known vessel; it may be a ticket agent or a slip. - The SS Friesland passenger (RZ-S05) is probably not Benjamin (medium-high). That ship reached Philadelphia on 18 Jan 1904, two months earlier. "Beni Zaslowsky" was marked married, but Benjamin first married at about 26, around 1910. And Beni's age of 21 points to a birth about 1882–83. - Lead (low): the SS Haverford left Liverpool 2 Mar and reached Philadelphia 14 Mar 1904, one day before Benjamin's date. On board were Itzik (49?, painter), Berl (22?, painter) and David (19?, tailor) Saslawer, last residence London, bound for New York (RZ-S25). The painter trade echoes the paperhanger witness B. Saslow, but no line names Benjamin. - Watch out: a second Benjamin Zaslow lived in Philadelphia (1920 census: born about 1888, arrived 1913, tailor). There was also a second Doris Zaslow, daughter of Max (Schaje) and Lena Zaslow.
