Jacob Rodilitz

1871–1950

AboutFamilyRecordsResearch notes

Jacob Rodilitz (Yiddish Jankel; Hebrew Yaakov, son of Yonah) was born on 15 March 1871 in Kovel, Volhynia, and was a Hebrew teacher. He went to America first, landing in May 1907, and bought his sons’ tickets one by one in New York. The family settled in East New York, Brooklyn, from about 1919 at 188 Malta Street. He was naturalized in February 1929. He died on 14 or 15 June 1950 and is buried beside his wife at Montefiore Cemetery, under a stone headed RODELETZ.

Jacob Rodilitz (R-020) and Mary (Miriam) Prozansky (R-021): Morris's parents

Death certificate of Mary Rodlitz (RZ-S02). The record says: - Died 14 Apr 1934 at home, 188 Malta St, Brooklyn. - Female, white, married, housewife. Born Dec 1869 in Russia; age 64 years 4 months. - In the US 20 years, all of them in New York City, so she arrived about 1914. - Father Abraham Porojinsky; mother Ida Projinsky, both born in Russia. - Cause of death: coronary thrombosis, with diabetes, hypertension and arteriosclerosis contributing. Physician Solomon Schneider, 229 E 91st St, Brooklyn. - Buried Montefiore Cemetery, 15 Apr 1934.

Conclusion: this is Morris's mother. Mary is the usual English form of Miriam. The maiden names Prozansky (RZ-S01), Porojinsky/Projinsky (RZ-S02) and Prosansky (RZ-S09) are one name. She lived on Malta St, the same street as Morris in 1915. Her birth year, 1869, matches the member tree.

Jacob's naturalization. US District Court, Eastern District of New York. The record says: - Declaration no. 70718, 13 Jun 1924: "Jacob Rodeletz", 53, Hebrew teacher, of 188 Malta St. Born Kovel, Volin [Volhynia], Russia, 15 Mar 1871; last foreign residence Kovel. Emigrated from Rotterdam on the Ryndam; arrived New York about 10 May 1907. Wife Maria. - Petition no. 82022, 16 Sep 1927: arrived New York 9 May 1907 on the Ryndam. Wife Mary, born 25 Dec 1869, Russia. Eight children: Max [Morris] 17 Apr 1890; Harry 20 Aug 1892; Meyer 5 Mar 1894; Benny 28 Apr 1896; Ida 19 Jan 1898; Essie [Esther] 4 Mar 1900; Louis 14 Aug 1905; Sarah 6 Feb 1907. Witnesses: his son Meyer, plumber, 188 Malta St, and Kalman Holland. - Naturalized Feb 1929, certificate no. 2898975. - Conclusions: Jacob came first, in May 1907, and Morris followed in December. He was a Hebrew teacher. the compiler’s tree is right about his birth date and Kovel. Ida's and Esther's birth dates here (1898, 1900) are earlier than their marriage ages suggest (about 1903, 1905). Ancestry suggests a "Jacob Rodeletz" in the New York City death index 1949–1965 and on Find a Grave, consistent with the tree's 14 Jun 1950 (not yet read).
