Itzik (Ike) Zaslow

1876–1940

AboutFamilyResearch notes

Itzik, who became Ike Zaslow, was born about 1876. He was probably the eldest of the three Saslawer brothers on the Haverford in 1904. A presser of cloaks in Brooklyn, he helped found the Bershider society in 1922 and is buried in its plot at Montefiore Cemetery. He died on 14 April 1940; his death certificate names his parents as Jacob Zaslow and Miriam Lesnov.
