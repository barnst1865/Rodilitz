Berko Zaslaver

d. before 1863

AboutFamilyResearch notes

Berko (Ber) Zaslaver is known as the father of Duvid, from Duvid’s marriage in 1847 and from the 1875 census, where Duvid is “son of Berko.” Duvid’s son Ber, born in 1863, was probably named for him, so he had probably died by then. He was Benjamin Saslow’s grandfather.
