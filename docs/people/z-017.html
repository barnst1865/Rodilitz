Herman (Hyman) Zaslow

1886–1958

AboutFamilyResearch notes

Herman (Hyman) Zaslow was born on 15 September 1886 and gave his birthplace as “Obodonka” on his 1942 draft card. He came in 1904 and lived with his parents in Brooklyn in 1910. He died in New York in 1958.
