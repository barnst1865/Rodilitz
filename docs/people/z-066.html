Bluma Zaslaver (Vaisman)

b. c. 1866

AboutFamilyResearch notes

Bluma, 22, daughter of Duvid Zaslaver, married Itsko Yankelevich Vaisman, a Tulchin townsman, in Tulchin on 10 August 1888. She may have been Benjamin Saslow’s sister. This is new research, not yet in the printed history.
