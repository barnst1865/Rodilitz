Harry Rodilitz

b. c. 1893

AboutFamilyResearch notes

Harry (Hersch), born on 20 August 1892, was the second son. A locksmith from Maczew, he landed in New York from the Cleveland on 12 June 1910. He married Fannie Bunder in 1919. He died in 1967 and is buried in the family plot at New Montefiore.
