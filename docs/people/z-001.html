Doris Zaslow

1926–2007

AboutFamilyResearch notes

Doris Zaslow was born in Philadelphia, the youngest child of Benjamin Zaslow and Catherine Saslow; the family lived at 2163 East Cambria Street. She lost her mother when she was eleven and her father when she was thirteen. In 1948, a secretary living in Brooklyn Heights, she took out a marriage licence with Theodore Rodilitz. They later made their home in Anaheim, California. She died on 25 February 2007.
