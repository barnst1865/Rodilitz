Harry (Hirsch) Lesnoff

b. c. 1862

AboutFamilyResearch notes

Harry Lesnoff, a painter who came in 1904 and lived near the Zaslawers in 1910, was probably the “H. Lesnoff” the family went to in 1906: perhaps Miriam’s brother or nephew.
