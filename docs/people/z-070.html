Khasya Dvoyra Nemirovskaya (Zaslaver)

b. c. 1830

AboutFamilyResearch notes

Khasya Dvoyra, daughter of Duvid Nemirovsky, married Duvid Zaslaver in Tulchin on 14 January 1847, aged 17. She was the mother of Ber (Benjamin Saslow), born in 1863, and of at least four other children recorded in Tulchin between 1859 and 1871.
