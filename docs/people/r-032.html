Yonah Rodilitz

d. before 1920

AboutFamilyResearch notes

Yonah (Jonah) is known from his son Jacob’s gravestone, “Yaakov son of R. Yonah.” Harry Rodilitz’s first son, born in 1920, was named Abraham Jonas after both of Harry’s grandfathers, so Yonah had probably died in Europe by then.
