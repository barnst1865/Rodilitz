Abraham Jonas Rodolitz

1920–1998

AboutFamilyResearch notes

Abraham Jonas, Harry’s first son, was born on 21 May 1920. He was named after both of Harry’s grandfathers, Abraham Prozansky and Yonah Rodilitz; his stone reads “Avraham Yonah son of R. Tzvi Hersh.” He died in 1998.
