Theodore and Doris

Brooklyn, 1948, and California.

Theodore Rodilitz and Doris Zaslow took out their marriage licence in Brooklyn on 7 September 1948, licence number 18800. He was 29, in radio production, still living with his parents at 537 Georgia Avenue; she was a secretary living in Brooklyn Heights. The licence gives Doris’s birth as 24 May 1927 in Philadelphia.1 He came from a family that had left the Kobrin district between 1907 and 1913; she was a Philadelphian whose grandparents had come from Podolia in 1892–93 and 1906. Doris had roots in Brooklyn too: her Zaslow grandparents had lived in Brownsville and East New York, close to the Rodilitzes’ streets, until their deaths in 1930 and 1931. How she and Theodore met is not known.

By 1968 Theodore was registered to vote in Orange County, California, and he and Doris made their home in Anaheim. Theodore died on 11 January 2004, aged 84, and Doris on 25 February 2007.2

Their daughter Sylvia married Victor E. Koik. Through that marriage the Rodilitzes and Zaslows of Kobrin and Podolia joined the Koik family, whose own history, from Estonia, is told in The Leoke and Koik Families.
