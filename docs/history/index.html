From Grodno, Volhynia and Podolia to America

The family history, chapter by chapter, with its records, maps and notes.

Theodore Rodilitz was born in Brooklyn in 1919 and Doris Zaslow in Philadelphia in 1926. All four of their grandparents’ families were Jews from the western edge of the Russian Empire: the Rodilitzes and Hausmans from around Kobrin and Brest in Grodno province, with roots in Kovel in Volhynia, and the Zaslows and Saslows from Tulchin and Obodovka in Podolia. They crossed between 1892 and 1925. These notes set out what the records, gathered in October and November 2026, show about who they were, where they came from, and how each of them reached America.
