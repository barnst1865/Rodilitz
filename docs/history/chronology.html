Chronology

The family’s dates in one list, 1855–2007.

Date Family Event
about 1829 Saslow Duvid (David), son of Berko Zaslaver, born
1847, 14 January Saslow Duvid Zaslaver marries Khasya Dvoyra Nemirovskaya in Tulchin
1855, 15 May Zaslow Jacob (Jack) Zaslow born, son of David Zaslow and Katia Goldberg
1863, 20 March Saslow Ber (Benjamin Saslow), son of Duvid and Khasya Zaslaver, born in Tulchin
1867, March Saslow Bessie (Batya) Weiss born, daughter of Harry (Aharon) Weiss and Molly Gold
1869, 25 December Rodilitz Mary (Miriam) Prozansky born, daughter of Abraham and Ida
1871, 15 March Rodilitz Jacob Rodilitz born at Kovel, Volhynia
1875 Saslow Jewish census: Duvid Zaslava of Soroki, 46, with sons Berko, 12, and Avrum, 3, living in Tulchin
1876 Zaslow Itzik (Ike) Zaslawer born
1880, 2 March Saslow Avrum Zaslav, son of Duvid, dies in Tulchin, aged 7
1885, 8 January Zaslow Benjamin Zaslow (Dov Ber) born at Obodovka, Podolia
1886, 15 September Zaslow Herman (Hyman) Zaslow born at Obodovka
1887, 26 July Saslow Aron, son of Ber and Basya Zaslaver, born in Tulchin; dies 3 September
1888, 10 August Saslow Bluma, daughter of Duvid Zaslaver, “from Dobroven,” marries Itsko Vaisman in Tulchin
1889, December Saslow Beckie (Jety) Saslow born
1890, 17 April Rodilitz Morris (Motel) Rodilitz born; later gives his home as Czerniany
1891, April Saslow Catherine (Beile) Saslow born
about 1892 Hausman Sylvia Hausman born, Grodno province
1892 Saslow Benjamin Saslow emigrates to America
1892–1907 Rodilitz Harry, Meyer, Benny, Ida, Esther, Louis and Sarah born, at Maciejów(?), Kobrin and Brest
1893, 16 June Saslow Bessie with Beckie and Catherine leaves Hamburg, the month Hamburg bans Russian steerage passengers
1893, 3 July Saslow They land at Ellis Island from Glasgow on the Circassia, bound for Philadelphia
1902, 15 December Saslow Benjamin Saslow naturalized in Philadelphia
1904, 14 March Zaslow The Haverford lands at Philadelphia with Itzik, Berl and David Saslawer; Benjamin Zaslow swore he arrived on 15 March
1906, 6 August Zaslow Jacob and Miriam Zaslawer and Ruchle land at Ellis Island on the Umbria
1907, 27 April Rodilitz Jacob Rodilitz sails from Rotterdam on the Rijndam; arrives 7–9 May
1907, 16 December Rodilitz Morris lands from Bremen on the Breslau
1910 Hausman Sylvia Hausman comes to America (year from censuses)
1909, 3 August Prozansky Mary’s brother Moishe Velvel Prozansky lands from Antwerp on the Kroonland, leaving her in Divin
1910, 12 June Rodilitz Harry lands from Hamburg on the Cleveland
1910 Zaslow Benjamin Zaslow and Catherine Saslow marry in Philadelphia
1911, 31 July Rodilitz Meyer lands on the Rotterdam
1911, 22 December Zaslow Sylvia Zaslow born, Philadelphia
1912, 2–3 July Rodilitz Benny lands from Rotterdam on the Potsdam
1913, 22 December Rodilitz Mary lands from Hamburg on the Pretoria with Ida, Esther, Louis and Sarah
1914, 26 August Saslow Beckie Mitchell née Saslow dies, aged 25
1915, June Rodilitz All ten of Jacob’s family together at 115 Malta St, Brooklyn
1915, 7 November Rodilitz Morris Rodilitz marries Sylvia Hausman at 41 Malta St
1918, 30 December Zaslow Edward Zaslow born
1919, 25 April Rodilitz Theodore Rodilitz born, Brooklyn
1924, 10 March Zaslow Benjamin Zaslow naturalized; his father-in-law is his witness
1925, 12 May Hausman Rose Hausman lands from Havana on the Ebro
1926, 24 May Zaslow Doris Zaslow born, Philadelphia
1929, February Rodilitz Jacob Rodilitz naturalized
1930, 9 July Zaslow Jack Zaslow dies in Brooklyn
1931, 14 September Zaslow Miriam Zaslow dies in Brooklyn
1932, 15 November Hausman Rose Hausman dies in the Bronx
1934, 14 April Rodilitz Mary Rodilitz dies at 188 Malta St
1936, 8 May Saslow Bessie Saslow dies
1937, 19 July Saslow Benjamin Saslow dies
1937, 5 September Zaslow Catherine Zaslow dies
1940, 29 February Zaslow Benjamin Zaslow dies
1948, 7 September Both Theodore Rodilitz and Doris Zaslow take out a marriage licence in Brooklyn

1957, 6 January Rodilitz Morris Rodilitz dies
