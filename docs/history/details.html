Rodilitz

Jacob Rodilitz (Jankel Rodeletz; Hebrew Yaakov), son of Yonah, born 15 March 1871 at Kovel; Hebrew teacher; arrived 1907; naturalized 1929 (certificate 2898975); died 14–15 June 1950; buried Montefiore. Mary (Miriam Reizel, Rose), née Prozansky (Pruzansky, Porojinsky), born 25 December 1869, daughter of Abraham and Ida; arrived 1913; died 14 April 1934; buried Montefiore. Her brother Moishe Velvel Prozansky (Morris William Rosen), born 1892, arrived 1909, died in Los Angeles about 1982.

Their children: Morris (Motel), 1890–1957, married Sylvia Hausman (1915). Harry (Hersch; Hebrew Tzvi Hersh), 1892–1967, married Fannie Bunder (1919); their sons Abraham Jonas (1920–1998) and Stanley (1927–1936), who is buried at Montefiore, like his grandmother. Meyer (Meier), 1894–1950, a plumber, married Henrietta Frankel (1932). Benny (Berl, Benjamin), 1896. Ida (Chaje), 1898, married Irving Hovelin (1927). Esther, 1900 or 1903, married Sol Feinberg (1927). Louis (Leib), 1905–1970, married Gertrude Kaplan (1934). Sarah (Sure), 1907, married Martin Gordon (1929), himself born in Kobrin.

Hausman

Joseph Hausman, died before 1925. Rose (Reise), née Shapiro, born about 1865, probably at Brest-Litovsk; daughter of William Sapir(?) and Hannah Katz; arrived from Havana 1925; died in the Bronx 15 November 1932. Their daughters: Sylvia (Sivia), about 1892–1952, married Morris Rodilitz; Fega, in Havana in 1925; and a married daughter, G. Weisberg, of the Bronx.

Saslow

Duvid (David) Zaslaver (Zaslava), son of Berko, born about 1829, a townsman of Soroki, Bessarabia, living at Tulchin in 1875, was very probably Benjamin’s father. His other children probably included Bluma, born about 1866, who married Itsko Vaisman (Weissman) in 1888, and Avrum, about 1872–1880.

Benjamin (Dov) Saslow (probably Berko Zaslaver), son of David, born June 1865; paperhanger; arrived 1892; naturalized 1902; died 19 July 1937. Bessie (Basche, Batya), née Weiss, born March 1867, daughter of Harry (Aharon) Weiss and Molly Gold; arrived 1893; died 8 May 1936. Children: Beckie (Jety), 1889–1914, married David Mitchell; Catherine (Beile, Katie), 1891–1937, married Benjamin Zaslow; Louis, 1893 or 1895–1965; Herman, 1896; Mollie, 1899. Six children were born by 1900, five of them living.

Zaslow

Jacob (Jankel, Jack) Zaslow (Zaslawer, Soslawer), son of David Zaslow and Katia Goldberg, born 15 May 1855; arrived 1906; dry goods merchant (probably the “Reverend” Jacob of the 1920 census, at 220 Livonia Avenue); died 9 July 1930. Miriam (Marjem, Mary), née Lesnoff, daughter of Herman and Rose Lesnoff, born 1848 or about 1854; died 14 September 1931. Nine children, seven living in 1910, among them: Itzik (Ike), 1876–1940; Benjamin (Dov Ber, probably Berl), 1885–1940, married Catherine Saslow; Herman (Hyman), 1886–1958; Ruchle, about 1889; and probably David, the third brother of 1904 (unidentified).

Benjamin and Catherine’s children: Sylvia, 1911; Edward, 1918; Mildred, 1923–1924; Doris, 1926–2007, married Theodore Rodilitz.
